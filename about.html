About the catalogue

A digital catalogue of manuscripts related to Johannes Versoris and his works.

The project

The Bibliotheca Manuscripta Versoriana is part of the SNSF Ambizione project Transferts des savoirs et création d'autorité: La diffusion européenne des ouvrages de Jean Versoris (ca. 1440–1500), led by Dr. Aurora Panzica at the University of Basel (Grant 216086, 2024–2027).

The manuscript collection contains detailed descriptions of codices containing works by or related to Johannes Versoris, including codicological information, content descriptions and scholarly notes. The person register documents scribes, owners and other historical figures connected to the preservation and transmission of his works.

The catalogue is a work in progress and is updated as research continues.

Features

Full-text search

Across manuscripts (titles, authors, dates, places, content) and persons (names, roles, affiliations).

Cross-references

Links between persons and the manuscripts they are associated with.

TEI encoding

All data is encoded following the TEI P5 guidelines for scholarly digital editions.

CETEIcean
