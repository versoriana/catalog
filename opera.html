Johannes Versoris's works and the manuscripts that transmit them

Each row is one work, with the number of manuscripts that transmit it as Versoris's own.

A number such as (+4) adds manuscripts carrying the same work but not as Versoris's: anonymous copies, copies attributed to another author, or copies whose ascription has since been rejected. Open a row to see them.

Parva naturalia, Ars vetus and Ars nova, at the end of the table, are groupings: their counts come from the works they contain, so they overlap with the rows above.
